Background

Berkeley trained product-designer, I specialize in UI/UX development and approach building as fluid & relational. I work on/in cognitive linguistics and apply this to both my creative and professional work.

Skills include coding in Python and R; training in neural network-modeling, chemistry, and economics. I design and ship digital experiences between fintech, e-commerce, and OTT services. With client work spanning the public and private sector, my client work includes: Numi Tea, The Product-Life Institute, Hopelab (NGO), SF Tech Council, The Schoolab, The School of Public Health (UC Berkeley), and The Paris School of International Affairs. It’s the continuous, iterative ebb and flow of product design that excites me - building worlds by language, technology, sensibility, texture & color.
