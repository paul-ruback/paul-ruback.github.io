Log

Broadcast your own station. Tune into someone else's. Write what airs.

Log puts a station in your pocket — broadcast locally, own the signal outright. Put your station out there for anyone close by or turn the dial to explore. Then tune out — write in plain text, saved locally.

For the kid who ran the school announcements.
