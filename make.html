Log

a pocket writing device built to hold your words

Log is a clamshell built around a simple fact: your words don't need an app, an account, or a phone to exist. Open it, type, close it — plain text, saved locally. Get it out by pulling the memory card or plugging in like a drive. The radio is the other half of the device: LoRa and legacy paging let a note reach someone else when you choose to send one. Quiet by default, broadcast when you mean it.

Spec
Body
~108 × 54 × 17 mm · clamshell hinge
Display
E-ink reader + memory LCD compose strip, interior lid
Input
Full QWERTY · voice-to-text secondary
Export
microSD + USB-C · pull the card or plug in like a drive — no app needed

LoRa + legacy paging · send a note to anyone, anywhere, off-grid
